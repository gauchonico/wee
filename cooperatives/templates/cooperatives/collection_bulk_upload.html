CSV Format Requirements

Your CSV file should include the following columns:

  • member_id - The member's ID
  • product - The product name (e.g., "Sunflower", "Maize")
  • quantity - The quantity collected
  • units - The unit of measurement
  • unit_price - Price per unit (optional)
  • total_price - Total price for the collection
  • collection_date - Date in YYYY-MM-DD format
  • collection_reference - Unique reference for the collection (optional)

For sunflower collections, the system will automatically link to existing planting allocations. For other products, new planting allocations will be created based on the member's land size.
